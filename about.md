Hello, I'm Brock Whitbread-Cole and I am a software engineer in the Office of Critical Services at California's Department of technology. I use this site to keep a public log of what I'm building (and maybe one day other things too). After finishing a degree in journalism that I never ended up using, I went through a coding bootcamp in 2014 and have been writing software ever since: front end and full stack roles in the private sector, a couple of years teaching at a bootcamp, and now software engineering in California state government. I'm currently in the final course of my computer science degree at the University of Colorado Boulder, where I'm building a self-directed capstone project where I'll learn Rust by building a game with it. You'll find weekly [project updates here](/categories/) for that project.

## Technical Skills
At work I build internal tools and reporting on Microsoft's Power Platform in a GCC High environment: Power Apps canvas apps, Power Automate flows, and the billing and usage reporting that runs on top of them. I also work in Go, building server-rendered web apps with templ and HTMX, deployed to Google Cloud Run, with BigQuery on the data side. In previous roles and through school, I have gained experience with Ruby, React, Python, and C/C++.
