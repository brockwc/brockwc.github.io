This week I narrowed down my options and decided that I am learning Rust by building a
game. Specifically, a terminal roguelike with a working title Path of Rust. I have wanted to
learn Rust and wanted to build a game for a while, and this capstone is the chance to do
both.

The project proposal is ready and going up today or tomorrow as a separate submission. It is
scoped to the course's roughly 45 hour budget: two weeks of ramp-up (all Rustlings
exercises, The Rust Book chapters 1 through 13, three small tested CLI programs), then a
minimal v1 game featuring a procedurally generated dungeon, movement and melee combat,
monster AI, hit points, and death and win conditions. This functionality will be supported
and backed by unit tests, clippy, and GitHub Actions CI to manage deployments. Multi-level
descent (traversing from one map to the next), items, and save/load are stretch goals, not
part of v1. As I learn more about setting up Rust projects I will get a repo started and
include it in my next update.

Next week: first half of Rustlings, Book chapters 1 through 9, the first CLI program, and
peer reviews of classmates' proposals.

No blockers right now.
