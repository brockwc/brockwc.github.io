This week was the first full week of the Rust ramp-up before I start working on my game
project. I split the time between Rustlings and The Rust Book, but ultimately enjoyed the
exercises more and spent more time with them.

On the Rustlings side I finished the intro, variables, functions, control flow, and
primitive types sections, and passed the first quiz. On the reading side I got through the
first three chapters and built the chapter 2 guessing game with Cargo. The guessing game
was a nice way to combine some of what was covered in the exercises and the early chapters
of the book in a single project.

I really like the Rustlings format: fixing compiler errors feels like it teaches faster
than reading alone, and it is easy to jump in and out of the exercises as I have the time.
I expect move semantics and error handling to be where it actually gets hard. Also, the
book is quite a bit denser than the exercises. My original plan had me at chapter 9 by now
but I am only at chapter 3. I am adjusting the split to keep Rustlings as the main line and
backfill chapters as the sections they pair with come up. I will likely keep reading the
book after I start on my game so I am not too worried about timeline changes.

Next week: the vecs, move semantics, structs, enums, and error handling Rustlings sections,
more book chapters, and creating the game repo. I will include the repo in the next update.

moved on to real projects?
